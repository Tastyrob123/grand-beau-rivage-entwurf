Switzerland Tourism

Member of Switzerland Tourism

Switzerland Tourism is the national marketing organisation of Switzerland and promotes Switzerland as a travel destination worldwide on behalf of the Confederation.

The Grand Beau-Rivage is a member and thus part of this network. You will also find the hotel on MySwitzerland.com, the website of Switzerland Tourism.

To MySwitzerland.com

Swisstainable · Level III – leading

Sustainability at the highest level

Swisstainable is the sustainability programme of Swiss tourism. It has three levels: committed, engaged and leading.

Level III – leading is the highest. A business only reaches it with a comprehensive, recognised sustainability certification that is verified externally by the respective certification body. The level applies for as long as this certification is valid.

The label stands for the entire hotel, not for a single offer.

More about Swisstainable

Club Grand Hôtel & Palace

One of Switzerland’s grand hotels

The Club Grand Hôtel & Palace was founded in 1984. It brings together Switzerland’s grand and palace hotels; more than 90 percent of these hotels are members.

Its aim: the name «Grand Hotel» should once again stand for what it meant in the Belle Époque, for outstanding quality and refined hospitality. To this end, the club preserves the heritage of these hotels, with the largest collection of historic hotel documents, more than 50’000 items.
