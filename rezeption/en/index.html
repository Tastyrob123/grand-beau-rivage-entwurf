The reception
in the chat.

At the bottom right of every page sits the reception of the Grand Beau-Rivage. Guests ask about the spa, Tea Time or the shuttle from Interlaken Ost and get the answer at once, from the pages and menus of the house, in their language, late in the evening too.

An AI works behind it. Whatever is not in the documents, it passes on to the reception.

Already booked, two more wishes.

Two guests have booked and arrive by train on 20 November. This is how the conversation goes on the phone:

  • The reception names the shuttle from Interlaken Ost on request and offers to request a table for the first evening.
  • Arrival time, time, number of guests and a name are enough; no form, no waiting line.
  • Both then sit with your concierge in La Réception, with a draft for the reply.
  • The house gets in touch personally; the reservation is made as today in your aleno.

What guests want to know.

Four conversations with the reception from 10 October, the answers word for word. If something is not in the documents, it says so instead of guessing.

A large house, many questions.

Two restaurants changing with the season, a bar with Tea Time, a terrace on the Aare from May to September, a spa with its own hours for pool and treatments, a shuttle from Interlaken Ost on request. Whoever wants to know this before arriving writes an e-mail or calls today.

The reception in the chat answers from every page of the website and from the menus of L’Ambiance, Le Vieux Rivage and La Petite Suisse, the wine list and the Treatment Menu. On the phone there is time for the guest who has something special in mind.

Book without leaving the page.

As soon as a guest asks about dates, the reception places the booking form in the conversation. «To booking» opens SimpleBooking in a large window, with the guest’s dates, the free rooms and the price of the day. Payment happens there as before.

Screenshot from 10 October 2026: two nights from 12 to 14 December, just as the reception placed them in the form.

Enquiries, answered in minutes.

The same assistant writes draft replies to enquiries for weddings, banquets and seminars: the guest’s mail goes in, a draft in the tone of the house comes out, with rooms, sizes and prices from the website and documents. What the house must decide is in brackets, below it for the team what is still open before sending. Nothing is sent until someone from the team has read it.

And tomorrow, with this weather?

The reception knows the forecast for the next three days for Interlaken, the Harder Kulm and the Jungfraujoch, plus the pages of the Jungfrau Railways and Interlaken Tourism. From this it puts together a day: spa and Tea Time when it rains, the way up when the sky is clear.

It gives operating hours with the source and the advice to check them before setting off.

What it does not do.

  • It invents nothing. If something is not in the documents, it says so and gives the number of the reception.
  • It promises no rooms. Only the booking system shows whether something is free.
  • It does not accept card or passport details.
  • It kindly steers back questions that have nothing to do with the house. Whoever asks a great deal in a short time is given the telephone number.
  • The privacy policy has its own sectionfor this, as a draft for the house to review.
