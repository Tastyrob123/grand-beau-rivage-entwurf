Hello

Thank you forstopping by.

Today I am not asking about the status. For your house I have designed a digital operating system for brand, website, guest journey and communication.

Lately the conversations between your hotel and me were only about the service contract. I believe that until then I never made clear enough what kind of project we were actually talking about. That the rebuild was declined I can therefore understand well: anyone who hardly knows Tasty Studios and only has a concept on paper in front of them will find an investment of this size hard to place.

So this time I show it to you to click through. The website is one part of it, 42 pages in six languages. With it come your four menus, newly typeset, 27 pieces of printed matter, luggage stickers, ten emails for your guests and an Instagram feed, all in one style.

Behind it stand the tools for your team. In Le Bureau you change a text, a photo or a price directly in the page, in La Carte you maintain the menus, and one button turns them into PDF and website. The reception in the chat answers your guests around the clock, in their language.

All of this has given me great pleasure; the Beau-Rivage is close to my heart. That is why I write no price beneath it. You decide which amount you feel comfortable with and what makes sense for your house.

Take your time looking through it, and get in touch once you have been able to talk about it. Warm regards from Koh Phangan!

Robert Reff
